Pre-Flight Process

Our Mandatory Safety Briefing

Every participant completes our 6-stage orientation before entering the ocean. Here is what to expect upon arrival.

01

Arrival & Valet Check-In

Check in at Pier 4 VIP Kiosk. Drivers present photo ID, complete digital health declarations, and deposit belongings in private digital lockboxes.

02

Custom Neoprene Fitting

Our lifeguards personally fit your USCG impact life vest, adjust straps, and equip you with rashguards, polarized eyewear, or wireless radio headgear.

03

Dry-Land Demonstration

Instructors demonstrate throttle trigger control, neutral reverse gates, wave turning techniques, and the emergency engine cutoff kill-switch lanyard.

04

Hand Signals & Radio

Review of universal maritime hand signals for "Speed Up", "Slow Down", "All Clear", and "Assist Me". Flyboarding & e-foiling receive waterproof 2-way radio helmets.

05

Harbor Escort Corridor

Vessels leave the marina in a controlled single-file idle speed queue (5 knots max) until clearing the 200-meter outer harbor breakwater.

06

Supervised Ocean Arena

Once in the buoy-marked open water arena, open full throttle while our 300hp rescue tender monitors the zone for 100% rider protection.

Clear Guidelines

Eligibility & Weather Flag System

Please review physical requirements and our Coast Guard flag condition policy prior to booking.

Rider Eligibility & Requirements

Jet Ski Drivers: Min age 16+ with valid Government Photo ID (Passport/Driver's License).
Tandem Passengers: Min age 10+ accompanied by adult driver. Parasailing tandem min age 6+.
Non-Swimmers: Fully welcomed on Parasailing, Speedboat, Catamaran & Banana Boat with PFD.
Weight Range: Max single rider 130kg (286 lbs). Combined parasail harness max 205kg (450 lbs).
Medical Limits: High-speed rides not recommended for pregnant guests or acute back/neck injuries.
Substance Policy: Zero tolerance alcohol/drug policy on motorized equipment.

Marine Condition Flag Standards

Green Flag • Normal VIP Operations

Calm waters, swell <1.5m, wind <15 knots. All 12 activities operate on schedule with full freestyle tracks.

Yellow Flag • Cautionary Swell

Moderate wind 15-20 knots or 1.8m swell. Novice riders assigned accompanied guide boats. Free rescheduling offered.

Red Flag • Full Marine Standby

High swell >2.2m, squalls, lightning. Water activities suspended. 100% full refund guarantee.

Safety Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions About Safety & Protocol

What happens if I fall off a jet ski into deep water?

When you detach from the seat, your coiled wrist lanyard automatically cuts the engine within milliseconds, preventing the jet ski from driving away. Your USCG Type III flotation vest keeps you buoyant with your head above water. You can re-board using the rear swim step or our rescue chase boat will assist you immediately.

Are there life jackets suitable for young children and toddlers?

Yes! We maintain an inventory of certified infant and youth life jackets equipped with under-leg crotch straps and buoyant head-support pillows. Our certified lifeguards inspect every buckle fit before allowing any minor near the docks.

What first-aid equipment is kept on site?

Our beach operations station is equipped with an Automated External Defibrillator (AED), medical emergency oxygen cylinders, comprehensive trauma first-aid bags, cervical spine immobilization collars, and VHF Channel 16 direct relay with Laguna Bay Harbor Patrol and Coast Guard Station 4.

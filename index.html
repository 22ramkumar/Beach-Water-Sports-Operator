Transparent Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before stepping onto the teak docks of Pier 4.

Do I need prior experience or a boat license to ride a jet ski or Seabob?

No boat license or previous water sports experience is required! All guests receive an intuitive dry-land training session, safety orientation, and an orientation lap before free riding in our supervised open-water arena. Drivers must be 16+ with valid photo ID.

What happens if weather or sea conditions are unfavorable?

Safety is our non-negotiable benchmark. If the Harbor Master or our Marine Director issues a Yellow or Red warning due to squalls, excessive swells, or lightning, you can reschedule at zero fee or receive an immediate 100% full refund with our Fair Weather Guarantee.

Can non-swimmers participate in water sports?

Yes! Activities such as Parasailing, Speed Boat Blast, Catamaran Sunset Cruise, and Banana Boat rides are fully accessible to non-swimmers because our high-flotation USCG certified impact vests ensure continuous head-above-water buoyancy.

What should I bring on the day of my reservation?

Bring swimwear, sunglasses with a head strap, reef-safe sunscreen, and a valid photo ID. We provide free secure lockers, life vests, wetsuits, water bottles, and waterproof phone cases on site.

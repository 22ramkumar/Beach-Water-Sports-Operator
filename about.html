The AquaSurge Philosophy

Born From a Passion for Waves & an Obsession with Flawless Service.

In 2012, AquaSurge was founded on a simple realization: while coastal water sports offered unmatched raw adrenaline, the typical rental experience was chaotic, impersonal, and compromised on safety.

We set out to create something extraordinary: a true 5-star private ocean club that fuses the thrill of 300hp supercharged craft with the refined bespoke hospitality of the French Riviera. Today, every reservation at AquaSurge is treated as a private expedition—complete with certified master instructors, complimentary 4K drone cinematography, biometric lockers, and chilled eucalyptus towel service.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our club, memberships, and private booking standards.

How does AquaSurge differ from standard public beach water sports rentals?

Unlike public beach operators, AquaSurge is a dedicated 5-star ocean club. We maintain a strict fleet cap, board directly from private deep-water docks, include certified master instructors with every experience, provide complimentary 4K drone cinematography reels, and furnish full VIP amenities including private dressing suites, chilled towels, and $10M maritime bond coverage.

Do I need prior experience or a boater license to ride 300hp craft?

No prior experience is necessary! Our USCG-certified master captains provide comprehensive 1-on-1 orientation and water telemetry briefings. For beginner riders, we configure our Sea-Doo craft to learning key mode and accompany your group with a safety tender. Experienced riders can unlock full Sport Mode in designated offshore open throttle zones.

Can AquaSurge accommodate large corporate retreats, bachelor parties, or private buyouts?

Yes, our VIP Concierge team specializes in custom multi-craft beach takeovers, private catamaran charters, and oceanfront cabana buyouts for groups of 6 to 60 guests. Custom group packages can include champagne catering, dedicated drone pilots, and personalized relay itineraries.

What is included in the complimentary 4K drone cinematography package?

All premium bookings include dedicated aerial filming by our FAA-certified drone pilots. Within 2 hours of completing your session, you receive an ultra-HD social-ready highlight video reel and full-resolution uncompressed flight clips delivered securely via AirDrop or cloud link.

Quick Answers

Booking & Reservation FAQs

What happens if weather conditions turn unfavorable?

Safety is our absolute #1 priority. If Coast Guard flags indicate swell over 1.8m or high winds, your session is automatically rescheduled to the next pristine slot or cancelled with zero charges.

Do I need a boating license or prior jet ski experience?

No previous license is required for standard rentals. Our USCG-certified instructors provide a thorough 15-minute dockside orientation and safety briefing before you depart Pier 4 marina. Riders must be 18+ to command.

What should I bring for check-in at Pier 4?

Bring a valid photo ID (Passport or Driver's License) and your reservation reference code. We provide all neoprene life vests, wetsuits, towels, sunscreen, and dry bags complimentary.

Can I modify or cancel my reservation slot?

Yes, you can modify date and time or cancel up to 2 hours prior to departure free of charge simply by texting or calling the Pier 4 Concierge Desk.

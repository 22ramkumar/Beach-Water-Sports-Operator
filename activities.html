Frequently Asked Questions About Rates & VIP Booking

Do you offer discounts for groups of 4 or more?

Yes! We automatically apply a 15% discount for groups of 4 or more participants on all single activities when booking together. You can toggle the "VIP Group (4+ Pax)" view on this page or select 4+ guests on the Contact booking form.

Can two people share one jet ski?

Yes, our Sea-Doo RXT-X 300 craft are high-stability 2-seater watercraft. A tandem passenger (such as a child aged 10+ or friend) can ride along for a nominal passenger fee of just $25 instead of purchasing two craft.

When do I pay for my reservation?

When reserving online, your slot is instantly locked with your VIP Reference ID. Payment is settled at Pier 4 check-in via Credit Card, Debit, Apple Pay, Google Pay, or Cash.

Marina & Concierge FAQs

Where do I park when arriving at Pier 4?

Complimentary valet parking is provided for all AquaSurge clients directly at the Pier 4 Marina Gatehouse. Attendants will park and secure your vehicle for the duration of your adventure.

How do I hail the marina dockmaster via marine VHF?

Inbound tenders and yachts may hail "AquaSurge Pier 4 Harbor Concierge" on VHF Channel 16, then shift to working Channel 68 for slip allocation and docking clearance.

Can I book large corporate retreats or wedding sunset charters?

Yes, our event team handles private charters of up to 40 guests across our catamaran fleet with catering, onboard champagne sommelier, and custom drone videography. Contact us directly using the inquiry form above.
